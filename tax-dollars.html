A statement, not a veto

No one can withhold taxes from a program they oppose; refusing to pay is illegal and leads to penalties. Even a president can't veto single lines of a spending bill: the Supreme Court struck that down in 1998. What citizens can do is tell Congress, which writes every spending bill, what they won't stand behind. Each objection here can become a drafted message to your members of Congress, in your words.
